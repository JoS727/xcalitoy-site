01 / WHAT IT IS

ONE ENGINE.
MANY ARTISTS.

Calitoy Stamp gives a musician everything they need to exist online in one platform: a 3D world fans can walk through, a merch store that prints and ships itself, and a vinyl pressing pipeline. No web developer, no inventory, no waiting.

  • 01
    3D WORLDS

    Each artist gets a street at night. Graffiti walls peel back into merch racks. A crate of records sits by the couch. A turntable drops the needle. Out back, a skatepark hides doors to other artists' worlds. Built in React Three Fiber, running at 60fps in the browser.

  • 02
    MERCH ENGINE

    AI-assisted logo design, Printful product matching, automated mockup generation, and Stripe checkout. You approve the artwork. The system creates the Printful products, generates mockups, and publishes to your storefront. Every order auto-fulfills. You never touch inventory.

  • 03
    VINYL PRESSING

    Connect your SoundCloud releases to Elastic Stage for on-demand vinyl pressing. Fans buy the vinyl, Elastic Stage presses and ships it. No minimums, no upfront costs, no warehouse. The record exists because someone bought it.

02 / MERCH ENGINE

FROM LOGO TO LIVE
WITHOUT TOUCHING INVENTORY.

The merch workflow is AI-assisted and human-approved. Generate a logo, match it to Printful products, generate mockups, approve the listing, and publish. Every order auto-fulfills through Printful. You never stock, pack, or ship anything.

Logo Generation

Generate transparent logo options from a brief. Preflight checks validate alpha channels, dimensions, contrast, and text accuracy before approval.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

FOUR STEPS
FROM IDEA TO INCOME.

01

CREATE THE BRIEF

Enter your brand, a logo prompt, your style, and what you want it on. The system generates transparent logo options for you to review.

02

APPROVE THE ART

Pick your logo. The system validates it as a production-ready PNG with true alpha transparency. You approve on light, dark, and checkerboard previews.

03

SELECT PRODUCTS

Choose from compatible Printful blanks, colors, sizes, and placements. The system generates real Printful mockups with your artwork applied.

04

PUBLISH AND SELL

Review the final listing, set your price, and publish. It goes live on your storefront with Stripe checkout. Every order auto-fulfills through Printful.
